TRAP 4Regression to the mean: the flight-instructor illusion

Cadets are scored on two landings (mean 70, s.d. 10). Instructors praise the best first landings and see them get worse; they criticise the worst and see them improve. Nothing they said caused it: extreme scores are partly luck, and luck does not repeat.

TRAP 6One number cannot describe a relationship

Anscombe's quartet (1973): all four datasets share the same means, variances, correlation (0.816) and regression line, y = 3 + 0.5x. Always plot the data.
