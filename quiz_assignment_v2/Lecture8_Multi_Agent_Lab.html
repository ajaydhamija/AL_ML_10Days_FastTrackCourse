DEMO 3Fictitious play: the average converges, the play cycles

Each round, both players best-respond to the empirical frequencies of the other's past play. In two-player zero-sum games those frequencies converge to equilibrium, even though each round's play is a pure action that an opponent could exploit. Exploitability here is the total gain available to best-responding players; it is zero only at equilibrium.

DEMO 4Who mattered? Difference rewards and Shapley credit

G(S) is the team reward when only the agents in S act (the rest idle). A difference reward credits each agent with G(all) − G(all without it). The Shapley value averages each agent's marginal contribution over every order of arrival, so the credits add up to the team reward even when agents' effects interact.
