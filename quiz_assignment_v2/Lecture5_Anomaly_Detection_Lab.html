DEMO 1Normal on each feature, abnormal together

A vehicle's engine temperature and fuel flow are standardised and strongly correlated. A z-score check flags a reading only if one feature is extreme. The Mahalanobis distance D² = zᵀΣ⁻¹z also asks whether the combination is plausible; under normality D² follows χ² with 2 degrees of freedom.

DEMO 3Catch a drift: Shewhart, CUSUM and EWMA

A gearbox vibration sensor reads N(0, 1) until sample 60, when its mean shifts by δ. Shewhart alarms on a single reading beyond ±3. CUSUM accumulates S⁺ = max(0, S⁺ + x − k) and alarms above h. EWMA smooths z = λx + (1−λ)z and alarms beyond its 3σ limit. DEMO 4Isolation forest: outliers need fewer cuts

Each tree splits the data at random points within its range until the reading is alone. Outliers are isolated in few cuts. The score s = 2−E[h]/c(ψ) compares the mean path length E[h] with c(ψ), the average for a random point in a sample of size ψ. Scores near 1 are anomalous; near 0.5 or below are normal.
