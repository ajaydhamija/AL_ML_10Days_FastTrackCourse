DEMO 1REINFORCE, with and without a baseline

A drone picks one of three ingress routes with a softmax policy π(a) ∝ eθa. Route rewards are noisy, with means 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 plus a common offset. REINFORCE updates θ ← θ + α(r − b)∇log π(a). The offset changes nothing about which route is best, but without a baseline b it inflates the noise in every update.

DEMO 2A Gaussian policy for a continuous aim

An aim point a is drawn from N(μ, σ²); the reward is −(a − 3)², so the target is a = 3. Each update samples a batch, subtracts the batch-mean baseline and steps μ and log σ along the policy gradient. Without an entropy bonus, σ keeps shrinking and exploration dies; with bonus β, the optimum is σ* = √(β/2).

DEMO 3PPO's clipped objective

PPO maximises min(ρA, clip(ρ, 1−ε, 1+ε)A), where ρ = πnew(a|s)/πold(a|s). Once the new policy has moved far enough in the helpful direction, the objective goes flat and the gradient becomes zero, so one batch cannot push the policy too far.
