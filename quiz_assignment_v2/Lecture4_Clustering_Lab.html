DEMO 2Choosing k: elbow and silhouette

For each k from 1 to 8, k-means++ is run ten times and the best result kept. WCSS always falls as k grows, so look for the bend; the silhouette (from −1 to 1) rewards tight, well-separated clusters and usually peaks at a sensible k.

DEMO 3DBSCAN: clusters as dense regions

A point is core if at least minPts points (itself included) lie within radius ε. Clusters grow outward from core points; border points join a cluster but do not extend it; everything else is noise. No k is needed, and curved or uneven groups are fine, but one ε must suit every density.

DEMO 4PCA: the direction of greatest spread

Two sensor features are measured for 200 contacts. PCA finds the eigenvectors of their covariance matrix: PC1 points along the greatest variance, PC2 at right angles. If one feature is recorded in larger units, it dominates PC1 unless you standardise first.
